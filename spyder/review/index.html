L2 — The boss framework

game/lib/boss.js:

// instance: update(dt, world), draw(ctx), drawGlow(ctx, light),

  • Spawning: rules.boss spawns the boss at room start, or at rules.bossAt seconds. Defeating it is an extra goal labelled with its name; the room's quota still applies to the regular spawns.
  • Hits: every weapon reaches the boss through the shared hit path. Sonic Pulse takes 25 % of max health. A hit freezes only the boss for 40 ms, flashes it white and shakes the screen 0.3.
  • Phases: spawns pause 1.5 s, enterPhase(id) is called, the screen shakes, a low roar plays (band-passed noise falling 240 → 90 Hz under two falling tones), and a note shows for 2 s: phase.note ?? "She's angry now.".
  • Defeat: slow motion at 0.4× for 1.2 s, and the boss's own death animation. The goal completes only when the boss reports done, so the makeover cannot start under the death. The base class shrinks and fades the stand-in over 0.8 s. A custom boss sets done itself; the runtime forces it after 4 s.
  • QA hooks: __spyder.bossTo(fraction), __spyder.bossPhase(id).
  • The stand-in in checks.mjs: 10 health, two phases. It is hit by all five weapons and changes phase. Sonic Pulse takes 2.5. On defeat the goal is not done at once, the sim runs at 0.4×, the goal completes after the death, and clearRoom() starts the makeover. No regular spawn happens in the 1.5 s after a phase change.

3. Decisions & Architectural Notes

  1. LightMap API Contract (lights vs glow):
  • The runtime calls room.lights(lightMap, live, t, mk) which takes 4 arguments and must return the flicker scalar flick.
  • The runtime then calls room.glow(ctx, lightMap, live, t, mk, flick, shift).
  • In master-bedroom/art.js, live.noise is seeded in createLive(), and flick = S.light.flicker(live.noise, t, 1.4) is computed and returned by lights() to avoid any undefined/NaN gradient values.
  1. Runtime clearRoom() Behavior for Extra Goals:
  • In game/lib/room-runtime.js, clearRoom() must satisfy roomIsClear(). For rooms with rules.extraGoals, clearRoom() marks all extra goals complete (rules.extraGoals.forEach(g => completedGoals.add(g.id))), guaranteeing that test automations (flowtest.mjs, checks.mjs) advance properly into makeover.
  1. Bouncy Bed Reach Calculations:
  • Standard jump budget: rise ≤ 190 px, gap ≤ 160 px.
  • The hat-box shelf is at y 520, which is a 260 px rise above the mattress (y 780).
  • Mattress bounce velocity at 1.35× normal jump speed (-1431 px/s with engine gravity 2800 px/s²) provides peak height h = v² / (2g) ≈ 365 px, with reach up to 270 px well within controlled jump arc.
  1. Zero Shared-Code Regression:
  • The addition of rules.extraGoals in room-runtime.js and hud.js was carefully isolated with guards if (rules.extraGoals) so rooms without extra goals execute with identical logic and rendering paths.
