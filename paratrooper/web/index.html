ParaTrooper

A port of the 1982 Orion Software game by Greg Kuperberg. The rules, the 18.2 Hz clock, the random number generator and the title melody are the original's, read out of the binary. The artwork is new.

Shoot the parachute, not the man. He drops like a stone and kills anyone he lands on — and since every shell you fire costs you a point, chaining kills is most of the difference between a mediocre score and a good one.

Three things end the game: four paratroopers massing on one side — they run in, climb on each other and reach over the sandbags; one landing on the gun base itself, which is fatal at once; and a bomb striking the emplacement. A bomb that lands on open ground is only an explosion — though it will kill a paratrooper standing there, and that man stops counting against you.

The original's controls were stranger than they look: one key started the gun swinging and it kept swinging, and another key stopped it and fired. You did not aim, you timed a stop. Press C on the title screen to play it that way.

Written from a byte-identical reconstruction of the original executable. How the game works, and how it was recovered, is written up in ../docs/. Original game © 1982 Orion Software, Inc.; this port ships none of it.
